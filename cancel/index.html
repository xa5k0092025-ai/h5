iPhone & iPad

How to cancel an iOS subscription

Use these steps for Apple Music, iCloud+, and any subscription you bought in the App Store. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Open Subscriptions
  1. Settings
  2. Your name
  3. Subscriptions

Cancel on your iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions. Sign in with your Apple Account if asked.
  4. Tap the subscription you want to stop.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription. You may need to scroll down to see it. If you see Cancel All Services, tap that instead.
  6. Confirm when asked. The page should show that the subscription will not renew.

If there is no Cancel button, or you see a red expiration message, the subscription is already canceled.

What happens after you cancel

You keep access until the end of the current billing period. Canceling stops the next charge. It does not remove access immediately, and it does not refund a charge that has already been made.

Free trial: cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you do not want to be charged.
